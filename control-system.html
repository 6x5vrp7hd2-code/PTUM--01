Product

Powertec Isolation and Document Control

Keys, locks, lockout boxes and safety documents for the AP office, held in one secure record.

Request a demonstration

Why it matters

On a busy site, isolations are controlled from key cabinets, paper registers and spreadsheets that drift apart under programme pressure. When the AP cannot see at once what is isolated, who holds each key and which documents are due to expire, permits are delayed and mistakes become possible.

The system brings those records together. Every lock and key is tracked against the document it secures, documents are issued only from accepted requests, and anything overdue or due for clearance is flagged before work is affected.

How it works

  1. DiscoveryA conversation about your site, its safety rules and how keys and documents are controlled today.
  2. DemonstrationA walk-through of the system using example data set up to look like your site.
  3. PilotThe system runs on one site, alongside your existing arrangements, for an agreed period.
  4. Set-upYour equipment, locks, lockout boxes, document types and people are loaded and checked.
  5. Go liveThe AP team is trained and the system becomes the site record.

Questions

Can it follow our own safety rules?

Yes. Document types, roles, lock colours and numbering are set up to match the safety rules in force on your site.

Who can see and change records?

Access is by role. Only those appointed to issue, receive or cancel a document can do so, and every change is recorded.

Does it replace our safety rules or the Authorised Person?

No. It is the record and control tool for the AP office. Responsibility stays with the people appointed under your rules.

What does a pilot involve?

One site, an agreed period, set-up of your equipment and people, and training for the AP team.
