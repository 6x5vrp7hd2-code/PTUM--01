Electrical safety and governance

Audits, safe systems of work and incident investigation for clients who need evidence that their electrical safety arrangements are being followed.

A procedure only works if it is followed. We audit permit holders, working parties and site practices against the client’s rules, report what we find and see the corrective actions through. On network rebuild contracts our project managers carried out four formal audits a month alongside daily checks on site.

We put a project’s safe systems of work and operating procedures in place, review and approve RAMS, manage arc flash precautions and PPE, and investigate incidents jointly with DNOs and Tier 1 contractors.

Experience

  • Electrical safety audits, reports and corrective action
  • ESSOW and SOPs implemented and audited on live projects
  • Incident investigation with DNOs and Tier 1 contractors
  • Process failures found for clients and replaced with working systems
