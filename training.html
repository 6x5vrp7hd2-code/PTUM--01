Training and mentoring for engineers working towards AP and SAP authorisation.

Powertec has trained client staff through to full SAP authorisation on several networks.

Training starts with what the person already knows. We find the gaps, plan the development to close them and mentor on site alongside the classroom work.

We have also built a web-based training platform for engineers moving into data centre work from other electrical backgrounds.
