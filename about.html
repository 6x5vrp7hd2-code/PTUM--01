About Powertec

High voltage operational and project support since 2012.

Powertec is run by a Senior Authorised Person who started as an electrical apprentice in 1996 and has worked on high voltage systems ever since. The company takes on work where switching, isolation and safety documentation have to be right first time, and where the client needs someone who can also run the project.

Career

  • 2026 to date SAP at 33kV on a hyperscale data centre programme in Europe
  • 2012 to date Director and SAP, Powertec Utilities Ltd. SAP services for DNOs, IDNOs, private networks and ICPs, and a £2m 20kV asset replacement programme as project manager and SAP
  • 2009 to 2012 SAP and senior project manager on HV refurbishment and rebuild schemes for ENS, HVDS, Freedom and Morgan Est
  • 2004 to 2009 SAP at Central Networks, now National Grid, with enhanced field control and EHV refurbishment work alongside National Grid
  • 1996 to 2004 Electrician, assistant design engineer and project manager at Haden Young

Qualifications

  • MBA, Business Administration
  • HND, Electrical and Mechanical Building Services Engineering
  • BTEC National Diploma, Electrical and Electronic Engineering
  • 2360 Electrical Installation apprenticeship
  • IOSH Health and Safety; GWO First Aid, Manual Handling, Working at Height, Sea Survival and Fire Awareness

Awards

  • House of Lords Award for the highest-scoring electrical apprentice in the AM2 final assessment
  • Student Electrical Engineer of the Year, Electrical Contractors Association and Edmundson Electrical
