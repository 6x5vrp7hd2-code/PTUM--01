Cables and ground-mounted plant

Replacement, testing and fault repair of underground cables, transformers, ring main units and LV boards.

Powertec delivers HV asset replacement programmes for Tier 1 utility contractors, providing the project management and Senior Authorised Persons and putting together the delivery teams of HV jointers and fitters.

Plant is tested before it goes back into service: switchgear and network testing, primary and secondary injection, and phasing. At the end of a programme we also clear long-standing faults on the surrounding network, so the client is handed a network running normally.

Experience

  • Transformer, RMU and LV board replacement in outdoor and indoor substations
  • Underground network rebuilds from construction to handover
  • Primary and secondary injection testing
  • Legacy fault rectification across a distribution patch
