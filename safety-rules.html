Safety rules

Electrical safety rules and procedures written, introduced and enforced, based on how network operators control their own systems.

Having worked under many different network owners’ rules, we know where they differ and where they agree. That makes it straightforward to give a site that has no rules of its own, or rules that are not being followed, a set that works.

The rules are only part of it. We also set up what makes them work day to day: the permit system and its registers, lock-out and tag-out, key control, emergency lock removal, warning and energisation notices, and the toolbox talks that explain them to the people on site.

Experience

  • Rules and procedures for private networks and construction sites
  • Permit to work, sanction for test and limitation of access systems
  • LOTO, key control and emergency lock removal
  • Working knowledge of DNO, IDNO and ICP rule books
