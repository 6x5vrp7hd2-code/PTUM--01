About Powertec

High voltage operational control, network delivery and electrical safety since 2012.

Powertec Utilities Ltd was established in 2012 to provide high voltage operational and project support to the electricity industry. Our work has covered six UK distribution networks as well as IDNOs, private networks and ICPs, from Kent and the south of England to Glasgow.

We take on work where switching, isolation and safety documentation have to be right first time, and where the client also needs the project run: people, plant, programme, safety and commercial control under one contract. Our teams include Senior Authorised Persons, Authorised Persons, HV jointers, fitters, project managers and logistics support.

We now also support data centre commissioning in Europe, bringing the operating discipline of UK distribution networks to a construction environment.

Selected work

  • A 20kV asset replacement programme in Yorkshire, worth around £2m, delivered for a Tier 1 utility contractor
  • SAP cover at 33kV on a hyperscale data centre programme in Europe
  • 6.6kV and 11kV overhead and underground refurbishment and rebuild schemes within DNO frameworks
  • A SAP training programme approved and used by EDF Energy Networks, now UK Power Networks
  • A lock-out and safety document control system, with iOS and Android apps, for data centre site set-up
