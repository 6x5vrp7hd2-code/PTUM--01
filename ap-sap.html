AP and SAP services

Authorised Person and Senior Authorised Person cover for HV and LV systems on distribution networks, private networks and construction sites.

Powertec provides Authorised and Senior Authorised Persons to network operators, Tier 1 contractors, private network owners and data centre developers. Our SAPs work under the safety rules of DNOs, IDNOs, private networks and ICPs from Kent to Glasgow, and regularly hold authorisations on several networks at the same time.

The SAP writes and carries out the switching, applies the isolation and earthing, proves the system dead and issues the safety documents. Nobody works until those conditions are in place, and they stay in place until the work is cleared and the documents are cancelled. We provide cover for a single outage or for a full site appointment.

Authorisations

  • SAP cover currently provided on a hyperscale data centre programme
  • Switching schedules written, checked and carried out
  • Dead-on, dead-off and live synchronisation of generators
  • Enhanced field control on six UK distribution networks
