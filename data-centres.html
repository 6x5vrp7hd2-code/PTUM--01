Operational control

Data centre AP and SAP support

HV operational control for data centre commissioning, from first energisation to handover.

Powertec currently provides SAP cover on a hyperscale data centre programme in Europe, controlling testing and commissioning on an HV network that is energised a section at a time while construction continues around it.

That means planning and carrying out switching on HV, MV and LV systems, setting the system and control boundaries, and issuing the safety documents that let each commissioning trade work. Isolation, earthing and staged energisation are arranged for VLF, load bank and UPS testing and for IST. Where testing needs and network safety conflict, it is resolved before anyone is put at risk.

Our teams bring the discipline of more than 20 years on UK distribution networks, where the same rules apply whatever the programme pressure.
