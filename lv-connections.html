LV new connections and reinforcement

New connections, substation upgrades and LV reinforcement, taken from survey and planning through to energisation.

We have led new connection and substation upgrade projects from the first site survey through planning, construction, commissioning and energisation, working on link boxes, LV boards and service cables to each network operator’s standards.

When an LV board or transformer is replaced, customers can stay on supply from a mobile generator. We are authorised to connect generators dead-on and dead-off and to synchronise them live, so the changeover is not noticed by the customer.

On circuits with recurring faults we introduced Kelvatek LineSIGHT and Sentinel monitoring, so intermittent faults were found and repaired before they turned into outages.
